Privacy Policy

Effective August 3, 2026

1. Information processed

Nahobess may process videos selected by the user, editable captions and hashtags, and authorization data required to connect to TikTok.

2. Purpose

Information is processed only to prepare content, obtain user approval, and export the selected video to the user's authorized TikTok account.

3. Storage

Video files and generated metadata are primarily stored on the user's device. Authentication credentials and access tokens should be stored locally and securely and are not intended to be exposed publicly.

4. Sharing

Nahobess sends selected content and required metadata to TikTok only after the user expressly initiates the upload. Information is not sold to advertisers.

5. Retention and deletion

Users can delete local videos, generated metadata, and saved credentials from their device. TikTok account data can be disconnected by revoking the app's authorization in TikTok settings.

6. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no system is completely secure. Users should protect their device, account, and API credentials.

7. Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the Nahobess GitHub profile.
